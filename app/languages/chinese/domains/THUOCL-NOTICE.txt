The source word lists were last updated in 2016/2017. IT document frequencies
come from the 2014-2016 CSDN corpus; finance and medicine use the 2008-2016
Sina News corpus described in THUOCL's README. DF means document frequency,
not a probability, current popularity measurement or a globally comparable rank.

TT9 selected 1,000 medicine, 800 finance and 800 computing/technology terms.
Only existing positive-weight factory word/reading pairs were eligible.
All terms are Han-only, 2-12 characters, with positive source DF. Existing
readings were segmented by the actual PinyinSyllables.segment implementation,
with exactly one legal segmentation for that Han character count. Ambiguous
or unsegmentable readings were skipped rather than guessed. All qualifying
readings of each selected word are retained; these subsets have one per word.

Every retained term string was reviewed for obvious topic noise, names,
malformed fragments and advertising/contact content. Generic news phrases,
unrelated people/places, media titles, stale unrelated stock names and clear
fragments were pruned. Legitimate medical/reproductive and financial-crime
terms remain vocabulary. There are no definitions, recommendations, contact
details or executable content. Factory pronunciations are reused without
claiming an independent manual certification of every pronunciation.

Source DF determines ordering within each subset. The stored weight is:
round(9999 * log1p(domain_df) / log1p(max_selected_domain_df)).
The original factory frequencies are not changed or copied into these TSVs.
Data selection does not delete any factory entry or alter personal words.

The selected TSV data are distributed under GPL-3.0-only alongside the existing
Chinese data notices, while preserving the THUOCL MIT source license above.
Application source retains its existing Apache-2.0 license. The complete GPLv3
text is in the Chinese distribution's rime-ice-license.txt. See the existing
RIME-ICE-NOTICE.txt for factory-dictionary provenance and source paths.

Preferred form for modification: medical.tsv, finance.tsv, technology.tsv and
source.json in app/languages/chinese/domains/. The matching source distribution
also provides the deterministic domain index builder. Source hashes and exact
selected TSV hashes are recorded in source.json.
