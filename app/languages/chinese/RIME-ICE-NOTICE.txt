TT9 changes: exact word-and-reading matches are assigned
round(9999 * log(1 + source_weight) / log(1 + 76938354)); unmatched or zero
source weights become zero. The 2026-10-28 reading audit appends 393 missing
readings for existing BMP characters using the same scale and pinned source;
all preceding 482,284 rows and weights remain unchanged. Of the resulting
482,677 word-and-reading pairs, 464,370 match the sources and 463,920 have
positive weights. The original rows and alternative readings remain available. The original
TT9 list was contributed by Vkiki2233: https://github.com/sspanak/tt9/issues/260
The additional SHUO / XING / ZHONG / LV readings and their source provenance
are documented in docs/dictionaries/pinyinWordlistReadme.txt.
The subsequent audit covers all 8,341 active source readings of 7,944 factory
BMP characters. It adds no characters or supplementary-plane readings and
does not enable commented-out source rows. The reviewed polyphonic pairs, raw
weights and source line numbers are recorded in polyphonic-readings.tsv;
tools/repair_chinese_readings.py verifies the pinned source hash and restores
missing exact pairs without replacing existing rows.

Build the word ZIP with buildDictionaryDownloads and the compact index with
buildChinesePrefixIndex. Use the source tree/patch matching this distribution,
not a later upstream download. Full original Rime dictionaries are not needed
at runtime and are not bundled.
